# MD-004 — CC BY double edge

Remixes can reheadline. DISCLOSURES don’t bind third parties.

**Remediation:** Accept risk, or add a short “how to quote us without lying” note; don’t pretend CC BY enforces tone.

## Self-check
1. Skim LICENSE + DISCLOSURES quote/remix language.
2. **Pass:** risk accepted in writing, or short “how to quote without lying” note exists.
3. **Fail:** assuming CC BY prevents hostile headlines.
