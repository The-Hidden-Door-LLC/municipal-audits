# Adversarial reviews

**Genre:** adversarial — hostile counsel / skeptical buyer / municipal attorney.

Not QA. Not praise with footnotes. The job is to land cheap and expensive shots so the public surface fails closed, not open.

## Scar and stitch (standing rule)

**Adversarial leaves without remediation are incomplete and must not ship.**

Attack + repair path + **self-check** (how they verify without us).

- Rule: [`SCAR-AND-STITCH.md`](SCAR-AND-STITCH.md)
- Method: [`CHECK-YOUR-OWN-WORK.md`](CHECK-YOUR-OWN-WORK.md)

## Severity

| Tier | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| `kill-shots` | Free gimmes. Fix or the product story collapses on contact. |
| `medium` | Real damage; survivable alone; ugly in a stack. |
| `survives` | What still stands after the cheap shots — keep visible so critique isn’t theater. |

## Targets

Attacks are also filed under `by-target/` so a Steward fixing disclosures doesn’t have to hunt date folders.

## Lifecycle

`open/` indexes unresolved leaves. Moving a leaf to `closed/` requires a Steward note: remediated / accepted risk / rejected as wrong — and a self-check that still passes cold.
