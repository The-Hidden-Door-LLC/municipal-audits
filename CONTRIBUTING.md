# Contributing

All contributions are subject to [`DISCLOSURES.md`](DISCLOSURES.md). Do not submit material that asserts criminal wrongdoing without Steward-accepted Finding nodes and counsel review.

1. Open or extend a case under `cases/` using FIPS + COG codes from `TAXONOMY.md`.
2. Put typed evidence in Aletheia records. Preserve conflicts.
3. Do not file FOIA/TPRA as a “fix” inside the repo narrative unless the Steward accepts that Action.
4. Propose changes; Steward accepts.
5. Keep PII out of public renditions.

Protocol: https://github.com/KarstenEvans/aletheia-protocol

## Adversarial reviews
Must include remediation and self-check. See `reviews/adversarial/SCAR-AND-STITCH.md` and `reviews/adversarial/CHECK-YOUR-OWN-WORK.md`.


Adversarial leaves must include remediation options (**scar and stitch**). Leaves that only attack are incomplete: see `reviews/adversarial/SCAR-AND-STITCH.md`.
