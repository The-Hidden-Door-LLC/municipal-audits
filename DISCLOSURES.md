## 2. Research and publication purpose

It is not an indictment, charging document, or enforcement action.

## 3. Evidence typing — read the labels

Materials here use Aletheia Protocol typing. Labels matter:

| Label | Means |
|-------|--------|
| **Evidence** | A bounded source or excerpt. Not automatically “true.” |
| **Claim** | An attributable assertion (e.g. Claim by the City). |
| **Hypothesis** | A testable possibility. **Not a finding.** |
| **Observation** | What was seen on a stated date. |
| **EvidenceGap** | Expected material that was not found. **Absence is not proof of crime.** |
| **Conflict** | Material tension between sides; both sides retained. |
| **Finding** | Only if explicitly labeled Finding and accepted by the Steward. |

If a statement is not labeled Finding, do not treat it as one.

## 4. No accusation of crime or corruption by default

Publication of budgets without payment-level ledgers, use of a public-records portal, fees, or residency rules, or comparison to another city’s transparency tools, **does not** by itself allege:

- criminal conduct;
- fraud;
- bribery;
- embezzlement;
- official misconduct;
- “corruption.”

Any such allegation, if ever made, will be explicit, sourced, and Steward-accepted. Until then, do not read it in.

- Public web pages change. Retrieval dates in evidence nodes control.
- Hashes (when present) prove file integrity only — not truth of contents.
- Readers should verify locators themselves before republishing or acting.
- We do not warrant completeness, currency, or fitness for a particular purpose.

## 6. Independence

Unless a case card says otherwise:

- Cases are not sponsored by the municipality under study.
- Mentions of Memphis, Shelby County, or other governments are **comparative publication practice**, not claims that one city “controls” or “taints” another.
- Trademarks and names of cities, vendors, and platforms remain their owners’. Use here is for identification and commentary.

## 7. People named in records

Individuals may appear because they hold public office, are listed on a city website, signed a public document, or are otherwise part of a public record.

- Naming a public official or published contact is not a personal attack.
- Private personal data of non-public figures should not be added to public renditions.
- Stewards will redact on good-faith request where privacy law or safety requires it and the material is not necessary to the civic question.

## 8. Petition portals and “available upon request”

A record that is obtainable only after a formal public-records request, wait, fee, or residency check is **not** treated in this project as the same thing as proactive public publication. That is a methodological position about openness — not a legal conclusion that the municipality has violated the Tennessee Public Records Act or any other statute. Legal conclusions are for counsel and courts.
